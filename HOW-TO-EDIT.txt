HOW TO EDIT YOUR SITE
=====================

QUICK EDITS (text, photos, links) — no code needed
1. Open your site in a browser on a computer and add  #edit  to the end of the address.
   Example: https://chasetucker.netlify.app/#edit
   (Opening index.html straight from this folder works too: open it, then add #edit and press Enter.)
2. Text: click any heading, paragraph, bullet, or caption and type.
3. Photos: put the new image file in the "images" folder first, then click the photo
   on the page and type its name, like  images/my-new-photo.jpg
4. Links and buttons: hold Shift and click the link, then paste the new address.
5. Click "Download index.html" in the bar at the bottom.
6. Replace the old index.html in this folder with the one you downloaded.
7. Re-upload: log in to Netlify, open your site, go to the "Deploys" tab, and drag
   this whole folder onto the upload area. Your live site updates in a few seconds.

Anyone who adds #edit to your address only changes their own screen. Nothing on
your live site changes unless you upload a new file.

BIGGER CHANGES (adding a new project, a new section, new animations)
Open index.html in a text editor such as VS Code. Each section is labeled with a
comment like  <!-- Plate: knee -->  or  <!-- LEADERSHIP -->. To add a new
leadership role, copy one  <article class="role"> ... </article>  block, paste it
below, and change the words.

The small italic captions under each animation are in the  data-stages='...'
line inside each section. Change the words between the quotes; leave the numbers.

FILES
index.html      the whole site
images/         every photo the site uses

PHOTO VIEWER
Every project photo opens full size when clicked (arrow keys or swipe to move through
that section's photos, Esc or swipe down to close). New photos you add get this
automatically. It switches off while you're in #edit mode so clicks still swap photos.
